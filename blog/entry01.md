# Entry 1 : Topic (Fashion)
##### 10/23/26

## Choosing a topic 

The topic I decided on for this project was fashion. I chose this topic because I always struggle with outfits and clothes in general. Being 5'10 and finding clothes that fit perfectly to my height and size. I also enjoy seeing how people use their styles to express their personality. I wanted to learn more about the tech industry can relate to technology. Some interesting topics include hot AI is used to design clothes, how online shopping has changed the fashion industry, and how social media can change fashion trends rapidly. Researching these topics helped me understand how technology makes fashion more creative and accessible. My favorite part is learning how designers use new technology to create unique clothing and bring their ideas to life.

## Engineering Design Process 

I am in the first stage of the engineering design process, where the main goal is to define the problem. The problem I am going to explore with my topic is many people don't understand how technology is changing the fashion industry. The next stage is to research how my topic can expand to technology. I plan to use articles and websites to learn more about fashion and also ask my peers how social media affects their fashion choice. This will help me understand the issue and learn from different point of views.
